Problem

The challenge was to design an end-to-end Buy New Card journey that helped users discover, compare, and purchase the right travel card while maintaining the trust, security, and simplicity expected from Mastercard.

Our Approach

Bringing the complete journey into one experience

Designing for a global fintech product meant every interaction had to inspire confidence while meeting strict regulatory requirements. Rather than treating onboarding, authentication, and card acquisition as separate problems, we approached them as a single first-time experience.

The approach centered around a few key principles:

  • Designing a guided onboarding that clearly communicated the product’s value without overwhelming first-time users.
  • Simplifying authentication while accommodating PSD2 (SCA), PCI DSS, KYC, and other regional compliance requirements.
  • Introducing the Buy New Card journey as a natural extension of onboarding, allowing users to discover and purchase a travel card without leaving the app.
  • Establishing a consistent visual language that reinforced trust, clarity, and Mastercard’s premium brand.
  • Building reusable patterns that could scale across future card products, markets, and evolving regulatory needs.

Working within compliance constraints meant many steps were mandatory. Our focus was to make those moments feel intuitive rather than procedural, creating an experience that was secure by design without compromising usability.
